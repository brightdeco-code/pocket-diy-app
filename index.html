📖 DIY words, explained

Primer

A first coat that helps the next paint stick or seals a surface. Choose one suited to the material.

Undercoat

A preparatory paint coat used before some finishing paints, especially on woodwork.

Cutting in

Painting the edges of a wall neatly with a brush before using a roller on the main area.

Keying a surface

Lightly sanding a suitable sound surface to help the next coating adhere. Remove dust afterwards.

Caulk vs sealant

Decorators' caulk fills small dry interior gaps before painting. Suitable sanitary silicone seals wet-area joints; ordinary caulk is not a substitute.

Mist coat

A first coat used on suitable new plaster. Follow the paint manufacturer's specified dilution and preparation; not every paint should be watered down.
